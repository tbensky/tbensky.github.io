Ok, so here's some news about London 2026, and keep in mind that there's nothing you need to do right now:

Applications are open! You can apply here, by clicking the "Apply Now" button!

But again, you don't have to right now. This is NOT first come, first served. It is not competitive. The application is merely open, and the admission cycle will not happen until the application closes in March.

24-Oct-2025

One of the (small) things I love about this trip is right when we arrive. You'll all likely land at Heathrow airport, and your first challenge abroad is getting to your flat. You can take an Uber OR the blue Picadilly tube line to the Gloucester stop. Personally, I love just hopping on the tube! Yes, with your suitcase and all! They'll be ready for your arrival at your flat (keys, room assignments, etc.)

The company that hosts us (FIE--the Foundation for International Education), wants us there on Thursday June 18, even though classes don't start until the following Monday. So...we all have about 4 days to acclimate ourselves to London, our new home! I love this time so much, because it feels like a bit of an extension to the program.

There will be a formal meeting at the FIE building (114 Cromwell Rd) where they'll give you a short talk, and your all important London transport credit, so you can hop on a tube or bus within central London for free. As a professor, I really enjoy seeing you all finally "over there" for the first time.

FIE usually has a couple of short tours planned around your new neighborhood (the area around your flat) at Hyde Park Gate, showing you good places to get groceries, coffee, and all. But one of the tour stop is of the grounds of Kensington Palace, where Kate and William live. It's sooo cooool walking to the palace, through those black wrought iron gates with gold highlights that lead into the palace.
